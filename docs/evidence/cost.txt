Notes.

The load balancer that the Container Apps environment creates was billed at
zero on this subscription, against meters whose names end in Free. The retail
price list quotes $0.025 an hour for a standard load balancer. Whether that is
a free allowance, trial behaviour or how Azure treats a load balancer it
manages on your behalf, the usage data does not say.

The charged meters also came in below the retail price. A full day of the
registry cost $0.0972 where the Basic tier list price implies about $0.167,
and the public IP cost $0.0750 where $0.005 an hour implies $0.12. Both are
around 60 per cent of list, which suggests a discount on this subscription.

The 22 September figure was restated. The export written on the morning of
23 September gave that day as $0.0791. The export written that evening gave
the same day as $0.1935. Usage data settles over a day or two, so a figure
read the morning after is not final.

The final part day, 24 September, is not included here. The stack was
destroyed at 11:47 UTC that day and the export covering it had not been
written when this file was captured. Adding a part day at the 23 September
rate would put the whole build at roughly $0.45.
